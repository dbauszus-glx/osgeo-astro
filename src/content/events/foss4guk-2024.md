FOSS4G came back to Bristol for a sold-out one-day regional conference at the
[Engine Shed](https://engine-shed.co.uk/) by Bristol Temple Meads, with 14 talks running
from mid-morning through to a social at a nearby venue in the evening.

Talks covered cloud-native geospatial, reproducible science platforms, satellite data,
flood risk analysis, field data collection and the growth of the FOSS4G project ecosystem.
All of them were recorded and are on the
[FOSS4G:UK YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FOSS4GUK).

Thanks to our sponsors — [CGI](https://www.cgi.com/) as primary sponsor, with
[Telespazio](https://www.telespazio.com/) and
[Astun Technology](https://www.astuntechnology.com/) as core sponsors — and to everyone
who spoke and attended.

The original event site is archived at
[uk.osgeo.org/foss4guk2024](https://uk.osgeo.org/foss4guk2024/index.html).
