FOSS4G:UK 2025 in Leeds is done and dusted. 180 delegates attended over 50 talks and
workshops across two days at [Horizon Leeds](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/), and we got
[great feedback](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12awtfG16wTJ51ZSk7V3bFC6we8wTS1-x/view?usp=sharing)
on the event.

Most of the talks were recorded and are available on
[YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLCvveKqdciOm-5Mfrppm1-bWmHZeUUwQo).

Huge thanks to all our sponsors, speakers, attendees, the staff at Horizon Leeds, and
the Local Organising Committee for making this event such a success.
