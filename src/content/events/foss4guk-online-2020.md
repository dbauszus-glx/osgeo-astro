With in-person FOSS4G events cancelled in 2020, OSGeo:UK put on a one-day online
conference instead — and it turned out rather bigger than planned: 600 delegates from 77
countries and 33 presentations across three parallel streams.

The event cost around £200 to run. Attendance was free with a suggested £20 donation, and
those donations raised £2,500 for OSGeo and [MapAction](https://mapaction.org).

Every session was recorded and the videos are on the
[FOSS4G:UK YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FOSS4GUK).

The original event site, including the programme and session abstracts, is archived at
[uk.osgeo.org/foss4gukonline2020](https://uk.osgeo.org/foss4gukonline2020/).
