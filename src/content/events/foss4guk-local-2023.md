A distributed one-day conference: eight local venues running their own programmes, joined
together for shared keynotes and panels. Around 250 people took part, and the event raised
roughly £5,000 for OSGeo:UK.

Venues were the University of Glasgow, Fraser House Hub in Lancaster, the British
Geological Survey at Keyworth, Maynooth University in Ireland, Geovation in London, the
UWTSD IQ Campus in Swansea, the Willis Museum in Basingstoke, and the Engine Shed in
Bristol.

Keynotes came from Helen McKenzie (CARTO) on "Getting People Excited about Geo!" and Ujaval
Gandhi (Spatial Thoughts) on "Building a Career with Open-Source Technologies", alongside a
panel marking ten years since FOSS4G 2013 in Nottingham. Sessions were recorded and are on
the [FOSS4G:UK YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FOSS4GUK).

Thanks to our national sponsors [Addresscloud](https://www.addresscloud.com), the
[British Geological Survey](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/) and
[Astun Technology](https://www.astuntechnology.com/), to the local sponsors at each venue,
and to everyone who hosted, spoke and attended.
