Our first distributed FOSS4G:UK, held on PostGIS Day across nine venues: Bovey Tracey
(Dartmoor National Park), Bristol, Cardiff, Glasgow, Keyworth (British Geological Survey),
Leeds, London, Manchester and Southampton (Ordnance Survey).

246 people attended in person and around 50 joined online — about 300 on the day — and the
event raised roughly £3,500 for OSGeo:UK. Three shared keynotes covered open spatial data
science, the history of QGIS, and PostGIS development.

All sessions were streamed and are on the
[FOSS4G:UK YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FOSS4GUK/streams).

Thanks to our national sponsors — the [British Geological Survey](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/),
[Environment Systems](https://www.envsys.co.uk/), [GEOLYTIX](https://geolytix.com),
[Locate Press](https://locatepress.com/), [Mergin Maps](https://merginmaps.com/) and
[Ordnance Survey](https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/) — and to everyone who hosted, spoke
and attended.
