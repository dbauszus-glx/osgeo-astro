The OSGeo:UK Annual General Meeting took place on **Wednesday 1 October 2025, 4:15pm –
4:45pm** (UK time) during [FOSS4G:UK 2025](/events/foss4guk-2025/) at Horizon Leeds.

## Minutes

- **Present:** Nick Bearman (chair), Ant Scott (secretary), Dave Barter, Al Graham, Tom
  Armitage (committee) and around 70 members
- **Apologies for absence:** Jonny Huck, Matt Travis
- [Minutes from previous meeting](/events/agm-2024/)
- **Accounts 2024-25** — these figures were agreed:
  - Total income: £3,577
  - Total expenditure: £4,844
  - Loss: £1,265
  - Balance at year end (end March 2025): £11,668
- **Constitution:** the
  [proposed changes](https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/uk/2025-September/001353.html),
  including setting the term of office to three years for committee/officers and increasing
  the size of the committee from seven to nine, were discussed and agreed.
  [Updated constitution](https://uk.osgeo.org/files/OSGeoUKConstitution-2025_signed_NB_AS_JS.pdf)
- **Election of officers:**
  - Chair: Nick Bearman (2 year term, till AGM 2027)
  - Secretary: Ant Scott (1 year term, till AGM 2026)
  - Treasurer: Json Singh (3 year term, till AGM 2028)
- **Election of committee members:**
  - Dave Barter (1 year term, till AGM 2026)
  - Al Graham (1 year term, till AGM 2026)
  - Tom Armitage (2 year term, till AGM 2027)
  - Jonny Huck (2 year term, till AGM 2027)
  - Dennis Bauszus (3 year term, till AGM 2028)
  - James Milner (3 year term, till AGM 2028)
- Thanks to Matt Travis for his work as Treasurer since 2019.
- **OSGeo:UK membership — how should this be defined?** A number of suggestions and points
  were noted. These will be reviewed by the committee, and any change will be put forward to
  a general meeting:
  - Membership of OSGeo should be a requirement, and members can also sponsor
  - Germany have a paid membership, on a sliding scale
  - Some are not in favour of charging
  - OSGeo membership is confusing — there are differences between the wiki and the website
  - Some people might want to be a member without being publicly listed
- **Communications update (including newsletter):**
  - We are active on Mastodon, LinkedIn and Bluesky
  - The [newsletter](/newsletter/) has 900–1,100 subscribers and a 25–30% open rate. There
    have been 10 editions so far, and there is a form to submit news. Thanks to Al Graham
    for running this.
- **FOSS4G:UK 2025** has been a big success — 180 delegates, 50+ talks and workshops, and
  positive feedback so far.
- **Future events:**
  - FOSS4G:UK 2026? This was not discussed specifically, but would be a possibility if there
    is a group that wants to organise it.
  - [Discussion on an international event](https://talks.osgeo.org/foss4g-uk-2025/talk/88DKSH/),
    2 October 13:30. This was held on the second day of the conference. In short, there is a
    desire to bid for FOSS4G 2027 for the UK in Bristol, and around 20 people are willing to
    get involved.
- **Funding of open source geo projects:** it is proposed to arrange a separate call after
  FOSS4G:UK, when finances are clearer ([GoFundGeo](/gofundgeo/)). It's likely that between
  £5k and £10k will be available for distribution.
- **Any other business:** there was a discussion about diversity in the committee and
  membership. It was noted that no gender information was collected in registration for the
  conference, and that there should not be an expectation that women should always have to
  lead on this issue. It was recommended that the committee consider ways of increasing
  diversity across OSGeo:UK.
