Two sold-out days of talks and workshops at [Dynamic Earth](https://www.dynamicearth.org.uk/)
in Edinburgh, with an ice breaker on the evening of the 18th, the conference party on the
19th, and a community sprint on the 21st. Tickets were £80 including meals and
refreshments.

Talks were recorded and are on the
[FOSS4G:UK YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FOSS4GUK).
