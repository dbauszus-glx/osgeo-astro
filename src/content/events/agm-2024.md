The OSGeo:UK Annual General Meeting took place online on **Monday 18 November 2024 at
1pm**.

## Minutes

### Members present

Nick Bearman (Chair), Ant Scott (Secretary), Matt Travis (Treasurer), Alastair Graham, Tom
Armitage, Dave Barter (Committee), James Milner, Paul Wittle, Alexei Schwab, Jonny Huck,
Catherine Clissold-Jones, Steven Feldman, Dan Ormsby, Ian Mayo

### Apologies for absence

Barry Rowlingson

### Minutes from previous meeting

The [minutes of the 2023 AGM](/events/agm-2023/) were approved.

It was noted that the conference insurance question needs to be followed up before the next
major conference is organised.

### Accounts 2023-24

- Total income: £7,182.58
- Total expenditure: £8,088.90
- Loss: £906.32
- Balance at year end (end March 2024): £14,000.90
- Current balance for AGM: £13,825.54

Some venues in FOSS4G:UK Local 2023 needed some subsidy, hence the small loss. A small
surplus is expected in the current year following a successful FOSS4G:UK South West event.

### Election of officers

All officers and members apart from Tom Chadwin and Joseph Seddon had indicated their
willingness to be re-elected, leaving one vacancy on the committee. After discussion,
Catherine Clissold-Jones was unanimously elected to fill the vacant spot, and Jonny Huck
proposed as a co-opted member, to be confirmed by the committee. Dave Barter volunteered to
work as co-Treasurer with Matt Travis.

The pros and cons of limited terms of three years for committee members/officers were
discussed. It was agreed that this was a long term goal, but dependent on enough members
being involved to fill positions. It was also noted that specific roles for committee
members are useful, particularly for new members. Additionally, it was pointed out that while
we have a constitution because we are required to have one to have a bank account, we can
alter it as we wish to better reflect how we want to run OSGeo:UK. It was suggested to
"de-formalise" the constitution as much as possible. The committee will discuss all of these
points further.

### QGIS domains — update

[qgis.uk](https://qgis.uk) and [qgis.org.uk](https://qgis.org.uk/) currently redirect to
[uk.osgeo.org/qgis.html](https://uk.osgeo.org/qgis.html), as discussed at last year's AGM.

### [FOSS4G:UK 2024 South West](/events/foss4guk-2024/)

Agreed that this was a very successful event, with a full house of c. 65 delegates. Tickets
sold out over two weeks before, and we could probably have sold 100. Delegates came from all
parts of the country, with a great variety of talks.

Diversity was still an issue in the audience, talks and committee, although all submitted
talks were accepted. It was suggested that short talks and online or recorded presentations
may help. A budget up front would also have been useful.

### Future events

There is some interest in organising FOSS4G Europe or the international FOSS4G. Paul has
done some initial research; for both we would need to express an interest as soon as
possible. 2026 is the first realistic date, though 2027 is also an option. Paul will follow
up.

Agreed that we would aim to stage a national event in 2025. A healthy reserve will be needed
to fund this — probably at least £8k. There has been some discussion about holding it in
Bristol, but there may be other opportunities. Al will ask for suggestions on the
[OSGeo:UK list](https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/uk/2024-November/001300.html).

It was suggested that a member of the committee should be a point of contact representing the
OSGeo:UK committee on event organising teams, to make sure events sit within the values and
finances of OSGeo:UK. Al volunteered for this role.

### Funding of open source geo projects ([GoFundGeo](/gofundgeo/))

All proposals were agreed:

- pgRouting Gold Sponsorship level: $1,200/£963 per year
- QGIS Sustaining Membership, small level: €1,000/£840 per year
- qgis2web — supporting Andrea Ordonselli to take on and develop qgis2web: £1,000
- GeoServer: £1,000
- Terra Draw, James Milner: £1,000
- GISRUK & OSGeo:UK GoFundGeo Award: £200
- OSGeo Sponsorship Silver: £0

Total: £5,003, against a budget of ~£6,000.

For next time, consider diversity goals and improving academic links, and encourage
UK-centric bids.

### Any other business

Agreed that the OSGeo:UK website needs a revamp — Al will review. Pages need titles, and
[Alexei will add an issue for this](https://github.com/osgeouk/website/issues/159). Links on
the website for the committee should go to OSGeo profiles.

Nick was thanked for his unstinting work for OSGeo:UK over the past year.
