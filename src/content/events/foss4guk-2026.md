FOSS4G:UK returns to [Horizon Leeds](https://horizonleeds.co.uk/) on **Monday 12 and
Tuesday 13 October 2026**. Following the format that worked well in 2025, we'll run talks
and workshops in parallel rooms across both days — around 50 sessions in total — plus AMA
sessions where you can put questions to the people behind the projects.

We welcome a broad mix of delegates from across the open source geospatial community:
developers, practitioners, researchers, consultants, public sector teams, students and
community groups.

Check out the [FOSS4G:UK 2026 site for more details](https://uk.osgeo.org/foss4guk2026/).

## Social evening

We're at the Howard Assembly Rooms Bar on the evening of Monday 12 October. Places are
limited and booked separately from your conference ticket at £20, which covers food and
your first drink.

## Call for papers

The call for papers has now closed. If you submitted a proposal you can still view it via
the submission system.
