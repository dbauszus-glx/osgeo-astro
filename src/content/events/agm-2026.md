The OSGeo:UK Annual General Meeting takes place on **Monday 12 October 2026, 3:45pm –
4:15pm** (UK time) during [FOSS4G:UK 2026](/events/foss4guk-2026/) in Leeds.

## Agenda

1. Apologies for absence
2. [Minutes from previous meeting](/events/agm-2025/)
3. Accounts 2025-26
4. Constitution: vote on changes to the definition of membership, as
   [discussed on the mailing list](https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/uk/2026-September/001455.html)
   and included in the
   [updated version of the constitution](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ScVWKMjXgbDMWWOWiV4CcmCYVCKSGHolSBc0Dzg8Sg8/edit?usp=sharing)
5. Committee elections
   - Election of Secretary
   - Election of two committee members
6. Diversity
7. Communications
8. FOSS4G:UK 2026
9. Future events
   - [FOSS4G 2027](/events/foss4g-2027/)
10. Funding of Open Source Geo Projects: [GoFundGeo](/gofundgeo/)
11. Any other business
12. Date of next AGM

The agenda is also published at
[uk.osgeo.org/agm/agm2026.html](https://uk.osgeo.org/agm/agm2026.html).
