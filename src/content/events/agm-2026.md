4:30pm** (UK time) during [FOSS4G:UK 2026](/events/foss4guk-2026/) in Leeds.

Present: Nick Bearman (Chair), Json Singh (Treasurer), Ant Scott (Secretary), Dave Barter, Al Graham, Tom Armitage, Dennis Bauszus,(Committee Members) and around 50 members

4. Constitution: There was a discussion on the proposed changes to the constitution, focused on changes to the definition of membership, as
   [discussed on the mailing list](https://lists.osgeo.org/pipermail/uk/2026-September/001455.html)
   and included in the
   [updated version of the constitution](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ScVWKMjXgbDMWWOWiV4CcmCYVCKSGHolSBc0Dzg8Sg8/edit?usp=sharing). These changes were primarily intended to formalise eligibility for voting,but also to encourage greater involvement by members. It was noted that there was a procedure for removing inactive members. The changes to the constitution were agreed with no ojections.
5. Committee elections
   - Election of Secretary: One nomination had been received from Ant Scott, and he was elected unopposed.
   - Election of two committee members: Three nominations had been received, from Juan Ginzo, Steve Karmeinsky, and Aderaw Tsegaye. After a vote, Juan and Steve were elected to the committee
   - Dave Barter and Al Graham stood down, and were thanked for their contributions.
6. Diversity
    - There was a wide ranging discussion on increasing diversity in the membership and committee. Suggestions included:
      - Reserving 50% of places on the committee for woment
      - Improving accessibility for conferences, for example by providing childcare, and adjusting timings
      - Increasing the number of discounted tickets (20 were sold for this event), or introducing a pay what you want ticket
      - Capturing data on the gender of members
      - Clarifying the role of committee members, including attracting non-developers
   - The committee was asked to discuss this and reccomend changes.
