The OSGeo:UK Annual General Meeting took place online on **Tuesday 11 October 2022 at
12pm**.

## Minutes

### Officers and trustees present

Nick Bearman (Chair), Ant Scott (Secretary), Matt Travis (Treasurer), Dave Barter, Tom
Chadwin, Joseph Seddon (Committee), James Milner, Paul Wittle, Seraphim Alvanides, Barry
Rowlingson

### Apologies for absence

Alastair Graham, Tom Armitage, Matt Walker

### Minutes

The [minutes of the 2021 AGM](/events/agm-2021/) were approved.

### Accounts 2021-2022

The Treasurer presented a summary of the accounts, and these were accepted.

- Total income: £0
- Total expenditure: £1,558.34
- Loss: £1,558.34
- Balance at year end: £11,765.25
- Current balance in the accounts: £16,676.18 (13/10/2022)

### Future events

Current focus is on [FOSS4G:UK Local 2022](/events/foss4guk-local-2022/).

Future events will be considered later in the year. Options include a traditional
in-person, single-venue event, if a group is willing to organise it, or a series of events
along the lines of FOSS4G:UK Local 2022.

After FOSS4G:UK Local 2022, delegates and the mailing list will be surveyed for their
assessment of the event and their views on next year's event.

### Election of officers

The current officers and committee are all willing to continue, and were elected unopposed.
The committee will consider whether to co-opt additional members after FOSS4G:UK Local 2022.
Joseph Seddon is a co-opted non-voting member.

### Funding of open source geo projects

The following decisions were taken on funding:

- Funding for the Patreon of Regina Obe: $60 per month to continue
- James Milner, development of Terra Draw: £800
- QGIS Sustaining Membership: €500 [£440] per year
- Lutra crowdfunding, point cloud processing in QGIS: £1,000
- North Road/Nyall Dawson, QGIS HTML formatting in labels: £1,000

A total of £3,874.11. The Treasurer will process these payments.

For the remaining proposals, the following was decided:

- Paul Wittle, Dorset Council, £200 — to be discussed in spring when Paul has more details
- Kevin Williams, Welsh Government, £2,000 — Log4j and GeoServer discussions, Tom Chadwin to
  follow up
- GDAL funding — Nick Bearman to follow up, in particular how much of the initial GDAL
  funding bid is left, and whether they are interested in a continued funding option

Potentially about £1,000 remains for allocation at a later date.

Possible funding for next year:

- OpenLayers, supporting a developer directly
- GeoServer
- QGIS integration with toolsets commonly used in UK local authorities, for example the use
  of Open UPRN from Ordnance Survey. Paul Wittle and Matt Travis to follow up, including
  potential funding/match funding from local authorities.

### AOB

There was no AOB.

### Date of next AGM

Agreed that it would be at a similar date next year, to be decided.
