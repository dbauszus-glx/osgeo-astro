The OSGeo:UK Annual General Meeting took place on **Thursday 19 September 2019** during
[FOSS4G:UK 2019](/events/foss4guk-2019/) at Dynamic Earth, Edinburgh.

## Minutes

### Officers and trustees present

- Jo Cook
- Steven Feldman (remotely)
- Matt Travis
- Alastair Graham
- Antony Scott
- Dave Barter

### Apologies for absence

None

### Report on activities

Jo reported on the activities of the past year, including:

- FOSS4G:UK 2019
- QGIS sponsorship
- Java code sprint sponsorship
- OpenLayers 6
- Regina Obe's Postgres/PostGIS development — Patreon sponsorship

A FOSS4G event could be held in 2020, dependent on volunteers stepping forward to organise
it.

### Officers

Steven Feldman was stepping down from the role of Treasurer, and a vote of thanks was passed
for Steven's hard work over the years. Officers were appointed/confirmed as follows:

- Chair: Jo Cook
- Treasurer: Matt Travis
- Secretary: Ant Scott

### AOB

GeoServer: a proposal for funding GeoServer to pass the OGC interoperability tests.

- Important for validation by government agencies
- To be discussed by the committee and a proposal put to the mailing list

Vote of thanks to the organisers of FOSS4G:UK 2019 for a fantastic event.

A reminder that training courses on OSGeo software can be added to the
[website](/training/). Only a limited number of organisations do this at the moment, and more
organisations are encouraged to do so.
