FOSS4G — the annual global conference of the Open Source Geospatial Foundation — comes to
**Bristol from Monday 23 to Sunday 29 August 2027**, hosted by OSGeo:UK at the University of
Bristol. It is the first time the event has been held in the UK.

FOSS4G brings together the people who build and use open source geospatial software from all
over the world: developers, end users, decision makers, researchers and students. Recent
editions have drawn well over a thousand delegates.

## Programme

- **Monday 23 – Tuesday 24 August** — workshops, 09:00–17:00 at UoB Humanities
- **Wednesday 25 – Friday 27 August** — conference sessions, 09:00–17:00 at the Wills Memorial
  Building and the Chemistry Building
- **Saturday 28 – Sunday 29 August** — community code sprint
