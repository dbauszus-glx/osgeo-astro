[As agreed at the 2022 AGM](https://uk.osgeo.org/agm/agm2022minutes.html):

- [Funding for Patreonship of Regina Obe](https://www.patreon.com/reginaobe/overview): $60 per month inc VAT [£638.52 per year]
- [QGIS Sustaining Membership, small level](https://www.qgis.org/en/site/about/sustaining_members.html): €500 per year [£440 per year]
- [James Milner, development of Terra Draw](https://github.com/JamesLMilner/terra-draw): £800
- [Lutra Crowdfunding: Point cloud processing in QGIS](https://www.lutraconsulting.co.uk/crowdfunding/pointcloud-processing-qgis/): £1,000
- [North Road/Nyall Dawson QGIS HTML formatting in labels](https://github.com/qgis/QGIS/pull/50848): £1,000
- Potentially about £1,000 remaining for allocation at a later date.

With thanks to the [RGS-IBG GIScience Research Group](https://geoinfo.science/) who have provided funding of £250 to support the above projects. If your organisation is interested supporting future funding, please email <osgeouk@gmail.com>.

Additional donations have been made previously. See the past donations further down this page for details of these.
