FOSS4G:UK 2026 is Coming to Leeds!

We are delighted to announce that FOSS4G:UK is back in 2026!


  • Where: The event will be taking place at the wonderful Horizon Leeds venue.

  • When: On Monday 12th and Tuesday 13th October 2026.

  • What: Two days of amazing presentations and discussion around open-source software and open data and community, all related to geospatial.

  • Who: Anyone with an interest in geospatial and open concepts.

  • Why: Because we all need fun and interesting things in our lives!

  • How: You can get involved by submitting a talk proposal, registering and (if you are able) getting your organisation to join the list of event sponsors!

Building on the success of last year's event, the conference will once again offer two full days of talks and workshops spread across multiple rooms, packed with open source geospatial content, discussion and plenty of networking opportunities. No matter who you are, this is the prime national gathering for the open source geospatial community in the UK.

Wider Geospatial News

GeoServer 3.0-RC is now available and this milestone is the direct result of a community effort. The crowdfunding campaign launched in September 2024 set a total target of 550,000 EUR and surpassed its community goal of 400,000 EUR in May 2025.


What's new in GeoServer 3.0

  • New context-driven UI — redesigned administration interface, fully responsive

  • Full-screen layer preview — improved map exploration experience

  • Modern Java stack — JDK 17 alignment, Jakarta EE, Spring modernisation

  • Stronger security — updated vulnerability management and dependency hygiene

  • ImageN raster engine — replaces ageing JAI/ImageIO-Ext components

  • Clean upgrade path — from GeoServer 2.28.x, no data directory changes required


This is a great piece of news, and what gives us the fuzzies is that we at OSGeo:UK have had a thank you from the GeoServer team for our support through GoFundGeo in 2024. See: https://uk.osgeo.org/gofundgeo.html#2024.


"I wanted to reach out to you personally on behalf of the entire GeoServer 3 team, to share an important update ... GeoServer 3.0-RC is now available.

This release candidate is the concrete result of the crowdfunding campaign you supported. Your contribution made it possible to move GeoServer 3 from a plan into a modernized platform ...

You can download GeoServer 3.0-RC directly from https://geoserver.org/release/3.0-RC/, or try it immediately with Docker:
    docker run -p 8080:8080 docker.osgeo.org/geoserver:3.0-RC

... For a more detailed overview of the release and the journey from campaign to RC, I invite you to read our blog post.

Thank you again for believing in this work and for being part of it".
